redhat-repositories.json is a derived subset for RHEL 8/9/10 product families.
Repository IDs are preserved and selected CPEs are converted to OSV ecosystem names.
Upstream timestamps, retrieval time and original SHA-256 are recorded in that file.
The mapping is bundled locally; no runtime contact with Red Hat is required.
